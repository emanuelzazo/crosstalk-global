India

Reaching the world's most unreached nation — one trainer at a time

The Challenge

1.2 Billion People. 90% Undertrained Pastors.

India is home to approximately 1.2 billion people — and is described as "the world's most unreached nation." Yet the nation shows increasing openness to the gospel.

According to Operation World: "Training Christian workers is an important need... 90% of pastors lack access to adequate theological training." The report adds that "new, creative ways for multiplying leaders must be developed."

CrossTalk partners with Cooperative Outreach of India to deliver training that meets this need — contextually, accessibly, and effectively.

Progress

What God Has Done in India

"The first cohort of 26 Indian leaders completed a 3-year program in 2019. Six graduates became Apprentice Instructors — training the next generation within their own culture."

New cohorts are planned for New Delhi, targeting 50 additional pastors and laypeople. CrossTalk's vision for India includes reaching not just New Delhi but the broader South Asian region through the multiplication of trained trainers.
