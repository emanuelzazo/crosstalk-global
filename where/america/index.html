CrossTalk America

Preaching is easy!

Or is it?

Do you feel the weight of having to “rightly divide the word of truth”?

And then deliver that truth with the transforming power of the Holy Spirit?

To people who came to church with very different backgrounds and concerns?

And doing so every week?

If you long to preach God’s Word more accurately and effectively, CrossTalk Global can help. CrossTalk Global provides the skill-based, results-oriented, continuing education that you’ve been looking for.

The CrossTalk Difference

Built Around You and Your Ministry

Five commitments that shape every cohort, every session and every coaching conversation.

Cohort-Based

We believe the best learning occurs within relationships, so we limit the size of our classes. Here you can develop strong relationships with people who share your passion and will enrich your ministry.

Faculty-Mentor Led

Our coaches are recognized experts who will walk with you throughout the program. And they will share their lives as well as their expertise along the way.

Culturally Aware

Our international expertise ensures that the way you preach will resonate with your culture.

Practitioner-Sensitive

We believe that learning is doing and won’t ask you to write papers and book reports. Your assignments will be integrated into and designed to assist your ministry.

Spiritual Formation-Focused

We believe that truly effective preachers first apply God’s word to themselves. We shape lives with Scripture while shaping sermons from Scripture.

The Program

CrossTalk’s Unique Program Won’t Interrupt Your Ministry

The program consists of six, one-week instructional sessions held every six months over 2½ years.

  • Class schedules ensure you will never miss a Sunday.
  • Six one-week sessions, held every six months
  • Expert personalized coaching every month
  • You remain in your ministry context throughout
Curriculum

The Six Sessions

From the Epistles to the Prophets — a full arc through the genres of Scripture.

  1. Bringing God’s Letters to Life

    Marks of a Biblical Sermon · Untangling & Communicating the Epistles · Bringing Ancient Ideas into Today’s World

  2. Changing Lives With God’s Letters

    Creating Effective Sermons from the Epistles · What Will You Do With Your Big Idea? · Deep Preaching: Learning to Learn from the Holy Spirit

  3. Sharing God’s Stories

    Understanding God’s Stories · Diversity Training: Appreciating the Variety of Biblical Narratives · Communicating God’s Stories with Power

  4. Navigating Challenging Narratives

    Discovering the Ideas of God’s Complex Stories · Skillfully Speaking God’s Stories in a Narrative World

  5. Rediscovering the Gospels

    Experiencing the Uniqueness of Each Gospel · Transforming Lives With the Stories of Jesus

  6. Speaking to the Heart

    Psalms: Preaching Songs Without the Music · Proverbs: Speaking Wisdom in an Age of Foolishness · Reclaiming Old Testament Law & Prophets
