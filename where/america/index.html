North America

Equipping Leaders Across North America

CrossTalk Global trains pastors and laypeople across North America to become effective biblical communicators within their local communities and churches.

While the need for pastoral training is most acute in the developing world, North America is not without its own gaps. CrossTalk's North American work equips everyday Christians to communicate God's Word with clarity, depth, and cultural sensitivity.

Our North American graduates often go on to support CrossTalk's international work — as donors, prayer partners, instructors, or advocates within their own churches and communities.
