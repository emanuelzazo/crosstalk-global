Vietnam

Hanoi, Vietnam

Training leaders for the Evangelical Church of Vietnam North

A Remarkable Story

200,000 Believers, 300 Pastors

The Evangelical Church of Vietnam North (ECV-N) faces a severe leadership shortage — a crisis decades in the making.

With approximately 200,000 baptized believers and plans to establish 3,000 new churches, the ECV-N has only 300 pastors. One pastor oversees 18 congregations. Another manages 10,000 youth.

Two factors created this crisis: radio-based evangelism left most believers without discipleship, and Christian leader training was illegal for decades, preventing pastor development entirely.

What God Is Doing

From Crisis to Multiplication

In 2013, the North Vietnamese government authorized Christian leader training. The ECV-N President formally invited CrossTalk Global to Hanoi in response. Their first three-year cohort completed training in June 2017.

"Seventeen pastors and leaders became Certified Biblical Communicators, reaching approximately 1,700 people collectively." — Vietnam Program Report
"One student oversees 200+ church members and supervises 120 additional pastors throughout his province." — Vietnam Field Report
