Equipping biblical communicators on the East African coast

The Need

80% Without Training

An estimated 80% of pastors who serve local churches in Africa do so without any formal training.

CrossTalk Global began its African work in Mombasa, Kenya, with the goal of strengthening the local church through culturally-aware biblical communication instruction. Our training equips local leaders to serve their communities with depth and confidence.

The need is enormous — and the opportunity is extraordinary. The African church is among the fastest-growing in the world, and equipping its leaders will multiply the impact of that growth for generations to come.
