CrossTalk in Eastern Europe

CrossTalk Global operates in three Eastern European cities, training leaders who will multiply the impact of biblical communication throughout the region.

  • Chernivtsi, Ukraine — Serving the Ukrainian church through biblical communication training despite the challenging context of war and displacement.
  • Iași, Romania — Training pastors and laypeople in northeastern Romania, one of the country's major cultural centers.
  • Oradea, Romania — Working with leaders in northwestern Romania in partnership with local evangelical churches.
