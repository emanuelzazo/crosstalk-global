CrossTalk in Latin America

CrossTalk Global operates in three cities across Latin America, led by Regional Director Dr. Stephen Gunn.

  • Havana, Cuba — Training pastors and biblical communicators in the Cuban capital, equipping leaders to serve the growing Cuban church.
  • Colón, Matanzas, Cuba — Extending CrossTalk's reach into the Matanzas province, one of Cuba's key centers of evangelical Christianity.
  • Panama City, Panama — Building on the strong evangelical foundation in Panama, training leaders who will multiply throughout Central America.
