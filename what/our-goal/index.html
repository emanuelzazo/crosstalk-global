Our Goal

What We Do

Ministry Medics, Not Neurosurgeons

CrossTalk Global trains competent practitioners — not academic specialists.

Think of the difference between a neurosurgeon and a general practitioner. Seminaries produce theologians — experts in a narrow academic discipline. CrossTalk equips frontline practitioners: people who can go directly to communities, sit with individuals, and help them understand and apply biblical truth.

Our goal is to provide competent biblical communicators across cultures who deliver needed training without the barriers of academic degrees, financial cost, or geographic inaccessibility.

"CrossTalk Global trains trainers. Gifted graduates mentor others within their culture, resulting in exponential growth."

What We Are NOT Offering

CrossTalk Global intentionally avoids several common approaches in order to remain focused and effective.

Academic Degrees

We do not offer seminary degrees or academic credentials. Our focus is practical, applied ministry.

Denominational Affiliation

CrossTalk is trans-denominational. We work across church traditions, focusing on shared biblical foundation.

Expensive Programs

Tuition is aligned with regional economic conditions. Cost is never a reason to be excluded from training.
