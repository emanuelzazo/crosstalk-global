Our Strategy

How We Work

A Five-Part Approach to Lasting Impact

CrossTalk Global's strategy is designed to be reproducible, accessible, and culturally sensitive — creating exponential growth in every culture we enter.

1. Adult Educational Model

Small cohorts of 12–20 students meet biannually across three years with consistent mentors. This mirrors Jesus's own teaching methodology — sustained, relational, transformational learning rather than one-off events.

The multi-year relationship between mentor and student creates deep formation, not just information transfer.

2. Accessible Education

CrossTalk removes barriers based on education, profession, or finances. Entrance qualifications emphasize character, intelligence, leadership, and teaching ability — not degrees or formal credentials.

The program is open to clergy and laypeople, men and women alike. Tuition rates are adjusted to align with regional economic conditions, ensuring cost is never a reason to be excluded.

3. Culturally Sensitive Communication

CrossTalk equips communicators to present biblical concepts in culturally-resonant ways, honoring both Scripture's original context and the cultural context of the audience.

Effective biblical communication is not simply translation — it is the art of helping people understand God's Word as if it were written directly to them.

4. Trainers Training Trainers

This is the heartbeat of CrossTalk's strategy. Graduates do not simply go home with new knowledge — they are expected to mentor new cohorts within their own culture, creating exponential expansion through peer instruction.

One trainer reaches 15 students. Those 15 students each train 15 more. The multiplication is real and measurable across every region we serve.

5. Long-Term Support

Annual regional conferences provide ongoing encouragement for trainers, re-certification, and resource support. CrossTalk does not abandon its graduates — we walk alongside them for the long term.

These gatherings also serve as a network for trainers to share experiences, challenges, and breakthroughs with one another.

See This Strategy in Action

Our results speak for themselves. Across nine countries, 291 students have been trained and 45 have become Apprentice Instructors.
