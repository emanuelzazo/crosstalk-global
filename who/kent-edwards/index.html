J. Kent Edwards

Founder & CEO

J. Kent Edwards

PhD, DMin — Founder & CEO of CrossTalk Global

Credentials: PhD – Intercultural Education, BIOLA University Doctor of Ministry – Preaching, Denver Seminary Master of Divinity – Tyndale Seminary Bachelor of Theology – Ambrose University

Kent Edwards is a tenured Professor of Preaching and Leadership at Talbot School of Theology / Biola University in La Mirada, California. He is also the Founder and CEO of CrossTalk Global.

Kent commenced pastoral work in 1981 and received ordination through the Christian & Missionary Alliance denomination in 1986. He has pastored and church planted across Canada and the USA for 35 years, successfully leading churches throughout both countries.

Prior to Talbot, Kent served on faculty at Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary (South Hamilton, MA) and directed doctoral ministry programs for 13 years across both Gordon-Conwell and Talbot.

"Kent's purpose in life is to change people's lives with the Word of God and help others do the same."

  • Effective First-Person Biblical Preaching — Zondervan, 2005 (Award-winning)
