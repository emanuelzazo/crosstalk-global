How Can I Help Teach?

Become an Instructor

Share What You Know

CrossTalk Global is always looking for qualified instructors who want to multiply their impact by training others.

If you have experience in biblical preaching, teaching, or communication, and a heart for cross-cultural ministry, CrossTalk may have a role for you as an instructor or faculty mentor.

Our faculty travel internationally to serve student cohorts — meeting in-person during the biannual residency sessions that are at the heart of CrossTalk's training model.

What We Look for in Instructors

  • Strong biblical knowledge and preaching/teaching experience
  • Cross-cultural sensitivity and a heart for global ministry
  • Willingness to travel internationally for training sessions
  • Alignment with CrossTalk's evangelical doctrinal statement
  • Ability to mentor and invest in students over a multi-year period

Already a CrossTalk Graduate?

If you have completed the CrossTalk program and want to become an Apprentice Instructor, we want to hear from you. Training graduates to become the next generation of instructors is at the heart of our multiplication model.

Contact us through the student portal or email us directly to discuss next steps.
