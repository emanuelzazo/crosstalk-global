How Can I Be a Student?

We Hope So!

CrossTalk Is Open to Everyone

CrossTalk Global works hard to make sure there are no academic, professional, or financial barriers to training.

We believe that all Christians should be equipped to communicate Scripture effectively. Our program is open to:

  • Clergy and laypeople alike
  • Men and women
  • Any denominational background
  • No prior formal education required

Tuition costs are adjusted to align with regional economic conditions — ensuring cost is never a reason to be excluded.

Entrance Requirements

We look for four qualities in prospective students — not degrees or credentials.

Character

A life of integrity and Christlike character that others can trust and follow.

Intelligence

The capacity to learn, understand complex ideas, and apply them in real-world contexts.

Leadership

Recognized by others in your community as someone who leads and influences effectively.

Teaching Ability

A demonstrated ability or strong potential to communicate ideas clearly to others.

"CrossTalk Global's objective is not simply to train you — it is to produce mentors who will equip others within your culture by forming and leading a new cohort of students. If the possibility of being asked to give to others what you receive does not excite you, then CrossTalk Global is probably not for you."

How to Apply

  1. Review the Entrance Requirements

    Read through the full student entrance requirements document to make sure CrossTalk is the right fit for you.

  2. Identify a CrossTalk Program Near You

    Check the WHERE section to find a program in your region, or contact us to learn about upcoming cohorts.

  3. Submit Your Application

    Complete the application form through our student portal or contact us directly.
