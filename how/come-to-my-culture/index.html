How Can CrossTalk Come to My Culture?

Starting a New Region

Bringing CrossTalk to Your Culture

CrossTalk Global believes "God's Word should be heard in every culture" and aims to "equip gifted, godly biblical communicators wherever they are needed."

If you represent a church, mission organization, or ministry network in a region not yet served by CrossTalk, we want to hear from you. We evaluate each potential new region carefully before committing to partnership.

What CrossTalk Evaluates

Before establishing partnerships in a new region, CrossTalk assesses several key factors:

Population Size

The target culture's population and the number of people who need biblical communication training.

Cultural Receptiveness

The local culture's openness to CrossTalk's approach to biblical communication training.

Government Openness

Legal and political context for Christian ministry and leader training in the region.

Local Cost Contribution

In-country percentage of costs that the local partner can contribute to the program.

Existing Partnerships

Potential partnerships with established evangelical organizations already working in the region.

What Partner Organizations Must Provide

  • A formal invitation for CrossTalk to work in the region
  • Assistance with understanding the local cultural context
  • Cooperation with other evangelical groups in the area
  • Help identifying and recruiting qualified students

The Partnership Process

  1. On-Site Investigation

    CrossTalk leadership conducts a field trip to evaluate the local context and meet potential partners.

  2. "Taste of CrossTalk" Seminar (Optional)

    A 2–4 day introductory seminar can be arranged to assess fit and build relationships before committing to a full program.

  3. Partnership Agreement Review

    Both parties review and sign a partnership agreement outlining roles, expectations, and commitments.

  4. Program Launch

    The first cohort is formed and the three-year training cycle begins.
