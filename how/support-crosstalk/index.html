Can I Support CrossTalk Global?

Yes, Please!

Join a Community of Like-Minded Partners

CrossTalk Global welcomes "a community of like-minded men and women who believe deeply in the power of God's Word." There are three primary ways to support our mission.

Way 1

Pray

Prayer is the foundation of everything CrossTalk does. We ask you to pray for:

  • New ministry opportunities and regions where CrossTalk can expand
  • Wisdom in equipping communicators across different cultures
  • Faculty mentors who travel internationally to serve students
  • Students serving in difficult and sometimes dangerous contexts
  • Financial provision for the full scope of CrossTalk's ministry

Stay informed through our newsletter and social media so you can pray specifically and effectively.

Way 2

Spread the Word

One of the most powerful things you can do is tell others about CrossTalk. There are many practical ways to spread awareness:

  • Share CrossTalk's story with your church or small group
  • Download and distribute our brochure
  • Invite a CrossTalk representative to speak at your church or mission committee
  • Host a fundraising event in your community
  • Request a seminar presentation by Dr. Kent Edwards for Bible teachers in your area
  • Follow and engage with us on social media
Way 3

Give Financially

Your financial investment in CrossTalk Global multiplies far beyond the dollar amount. Every gift supports training that changes lives for eternity.

$3,600
Sponsors one international student for a full year of training
$5,000
Funds an exploratory trip to evaluate a potential new ministry region
Any Amount
General donations support CrossTalk's operations, training materials, and faculty
